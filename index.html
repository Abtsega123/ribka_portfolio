Research & Experience

Master's Thesis Research — Adam Lab, The George Washington University Sept 2025 – May 2026
Washington, DC · Supported by the U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Science
  • Investigated whether a substantially reduced recurrent circuit retains associative memory function, characterizing cell assembly formation under spike-timing-dependent plasticity and pattern completion from partial cues, with short-term plasticity modeled using the Tsodyks–Markram formalism.
  • Designed a four-term composite objective quantifying completion, separation, precision, and recall error, built to rule out degenerate solutions such as indiscriminate or suppressed firing, and optimized coupled model parameters with derivative-free evolutionary search.
  • Analyzed spike rasters, synaptic weight matrices, membrane voltage, and input currents across cued, non-cued, and off-target populations. Achieved 91% peak reconstruction accuracy, with recall error falling to zero at 75% cue coverage.
  • Traced divergent GPU vs CPU conductance behavior for identical network configurations through the CARLsim C++/CUDA source.
  • Argued the resulting event-driven, local-learning architecture is tractable for neuromorphic hardware implementation.
AI Researcher — NEOALI Aug 2023 – Jun 2024

  • Fine-tuned Llama 3 on Korean dialogue data and integrated retrieval-augmented completion for an augmentative and alternative communication application serving users with severe communication impairments.
  • Built and deployed the production system end to end: Python backend with FastAPI and Starlette WebSocket, WebRTC for real-time audio, MongoDB, GCP deployment, and a cross-platform React Native frontend.

Contract Researcher — KAIST Institute for AI, Brain Science Research Center Apr 2022 – Apr 2023
Daejeon, South Korea
  • Built an end-to-end speech emotion recognition system combining top-down and bottom-up attention, improving classification accuracy on the IEMOCAP benchmark.
  • Benchmarked hand-designed spectral features (MFCC, Mel-spectrogram) against learned representations from large pretrained speech models, and extended the system to multimodal emotion recognition with facial-landmark fusion.
  • Designed a full logarithmic filterbank, analogous to the mel filterbank, permitting pitch shifting by remapping spectrogram frequency-bin indices, applied to voice conversion and text-to-speech.
